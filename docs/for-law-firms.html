How we work with firms

Attorney-controlled, not AI-run

This is a business-improvement engagement, not legal advice or automated legal content. The same boundaries apply to both services.

Attorneys keep final authority

AI supports research, drafting and analysis. Attorneys retain final authority over accuracy and claims, and every substantive page or template keeps a named reviewer and a last-reviewed date.

Client information stays out of unapproved tools

Client and prospective-client information is never placed into AI tools your firm has not approved. Where a firm uses chat or automated intake, we review it against a strict boundary — routing and scheduling, not advice.

No promises nobody can keep

We do not promise rankings, lead volume or revenue outcomes that no vendor controls. Recommendations are checked against the advertising and professional-responsibility rules of the states you actually practise in, and raised as observations for your attorney's review.
