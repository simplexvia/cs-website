About Simplex Via

Two decades at IBM helping companies ship faster, cleaner, and cheaper.
Now doing the same for small business, with AI.

Simplex Via was founded by Steven Cotugno — over thirty years building software, and two decades as an IBM managing consultant helping organizations adopt new technology and get a real return on it. Today that technology is AI. The job hasn't changed: shorter time to market, higher quality, lower cost to operate.

Most recently, as a principal technologist for cloud and AI platforms, I co-led an enterprise AI proof-of-concept spanning 11 use cases and more than 25 engineers — and personally delivered the output: architecture documentation, 247 automated tests, and AI-assisted triage guides that cut the hours engineers spent chasing blocking issues.

That's the same idea at a different scale. A small business doesn't need 247 tests. It needs the quote out today instead of Thursday, the same answer every time, and four hours a week back. Same method, same three outcomes — I call it the ROI Compass — just pointed at your business instead of a product line.

The ROI Compass

Faster Time to Market

Proposals, quotes, and client deliverables out in hours instead of days. Speed is the small-business advantage — AI is how you widen it.

Higher Quality, Consistently

The same standard on your busiest week as your slowest. Fewer errors, fewer rewrites, fewer things falling through.

Lower Cost to Operate

Hours of manual work off your team's plate without adding headcount — and less spend on tools nobody uses.

None of it comes from picking the best tool. It comes from sequencing the work — find where the time and money are actually going, prove the value on something small, then scale only what worked. At IBM the standard was a working solution in front of the client in six to eight weeks. Not a slide deck. Something that ran. That part hasn't changed either.

That work ran across more than 25 industries — which turns out to be the most useful thing about it. Different products, same bottlenecks: the handoff nobody owns, the report that takes a day to assemble, the quote that sits in someone's inbox. Whatever your business does, the problem slowing it down is almost certainly one I've watched somebody else solve.
