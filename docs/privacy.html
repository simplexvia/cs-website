Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

The short version. This site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. Nothing is collected about you unless you choose to send it — by writing to us, booking a call, taking the self-assessment, or paying for something. We do not sell, rent or trade any of it, and we will delete what we hold on request.

This policy explains what Simplex Via LLC does with information that reaches us through simplexvia.com and through the services we sell.

1. What this site collects on its own

Effectively nothing. There is no Google Analytics tag, no advertising or remarketing pixel, no heatmap recorder and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to. The site sets no cookies to identify or follow you.

Two mechanical exceptions, stated because they are true rather than because anyone would notice them:

  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web server, it records standard request data — IP address, timestamp, the page requested, and the browser's self-description — as part of delivering the page and defending against abuse. Those logs belong to the host. Simplex Via does not receive, read or analyse them.
  • A draft saved in your own browser. One interactive page stores your partial answers in your browser's local storage so a refresh does not lose them. That text stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and disappears when you clear your browser data.

2. What you give us on purpose

Everything else in this policy is information you chose to hand over. Specifically:

3. What we do with it

We use it to reply to you, to schedule and hold the conversation, to produce and deliver the work you have asked for, to invoice, and to keep the business records the law requires us to keep. That is the whole list.

What we do not do, stated plainly because a policy that only lists permissions is not telling you much:

  • We do not sell, rent, trade or broker your information to anyone.
  • We do not run advertising, so nothing is shared with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list because you contacted us. If we ever start one, you will have to ask to join it.
  • We do not publish a client's name, logo or results as a reference without asking first.

5. Client material, confidentiality and AI

Simplex Via uses AI tools to do its work, and client material is sometimes processed with them. What follows is a description of the practice rather than a promise about it, because the terms of the companies that build these tools are theirs to set and to change, not ours.

  • Identifying details — names, addresses, account numbers, anything that points at a particular person — are removed wherever the work does not require them.
  • Where a tool offers a setting that keeps content out of model training, that setting is turned on.
  • Client material is not pasted into free, public chatbots.

What cannot honestly be guaranteed is what a third-party provider does inside its own systems, or that its terms will stay as they are. Anyone who guarantees that to you about a tool they do not own is overstating it. If your work needs a binding commitment on this point — and for regulated work it may — say so before the engagement starts. It then goes into the written agreement, where it is worth something.

Anything you share with us in the course of an engagement is treated as confidential. We use it to do your work, and for nothing else.

6. How long we keep things

  • An enquiry that goes nowhere: deleted within about twelve months.
  • Self-assessment responses: kept while they are useful to a live conversation, then deleted.
  • Client records, invoices and tax records: kept as long as Arizona and federal law require, which is generally seven years.
  • Client work product and materials: kept for the life of the relationship and returned or deleted on request afterwards, subject to the records above.

7. Security, honestly described

Accounts are protected with strong, unique credentials and multi-factor authentication where the service supports it. Devices are encrypted. Access is limited to the people who need it, which at present is one person.

No one can promise that a system cannot be breached, and a policy that promises it is lying to you. If a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you, tell you what was involved, and tell you what we are doing about it.

8. Your choices

Write to info@simplexvia.com and you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • send you a copy of it;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, except where we are legally required to keep it.

Whether or not the law where you live grants those rights, you have them here. We will respond within thirty days.

9. Children

This is a service sold to businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and it will be deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting current clients will be communicated directly rather than left to be discovered.
