How it works: one fact, one file, a source and a signature

Your knowledge becomes a folder of short, plain-text files — one per topic — in the Open Knowledge Format, an open-source specification published by Google Cloud. Any AI tool can read them. So can you, in any text editor. Each file says where its facts came from, when it last changed, and who confirmed it.

policies/refunds.md
---
type: Policy 1
title: Refunds
description: When we refund, how fast, and who decides.
sources: 2
  - resource: "meeting:2026-08-14"
    title: Owner walkthrough
verified: { by: "human:owner", at: "2026-08-20" } 3
stale_after: "2027-02-20" 4
---
# Refunds 5

Full refund within 30 days, no questions.
After 30 days: the owner decides, case by case.
**Never** promise a refund by email before
the owner has seen the order.
An illustrative file, not a real client's.
1

One topic per file

This file covers one thing — refunds. type: Policy says what kind of topic it is, and the description sums it up in a line. Pricing, a product or a customer type would each get a file of their own.

2

Every fact has a source

sources shows where it came from: the owner walkthrough on 14 August. You can always check a fact against where it was said.

3

A person signs it off

verified records that the owner confirmed it on 20 August. Until someone you name signs off, a fact is labelled unconfirmed.

4

It knows when it's out of date

stale_after flags the policy for a re-check on 20 February. Prices, certifications and contacts get the same date.

5

The rule, in plain words

Below the header is the policy itself — including what never happens. That’s the line an AI tool reads before it answers a customer.

Propose, then approve

The AI does the upkeep. You decide what's true.

An AI agent reads what's new — documents, meeting notes, anything you drop in a folder — and drafts the changes. It never confirms its own work.

Talk to Us About Fit
  • New or changed facts arrive as drafts, with their source, for a named person to approve.
  • Nothing is deleted. A replaced fact is kept, marked as no longer current, and linked to what replaced it.
  • A weekly check reports what's out of date, what contradicts itself, and what has no source.
  • Payment details, credentials and anything you mark restricted stay out of it.
  • Medical, legal, tax and financial matters are recorded as your professional said them — the AI never advises on them.

Questions

Is this a chatbot?

No. It's what a chatbot, an email assistant or any other AI tool reads from. You can use it with the AI tools you already have.

What is the Open Knowledge Format?

An open-source specification, published by Google Cloud under the Apache 2.0 licence, for writing knowledge as a folder of plain markdown files with a small header on each. Because it's open, your files aren't tied to us or to any vendor.

Who owns it?

You do, from the first file. It lives in a folder or repository you control. If you stop working with us, it keeps working.

Does the AI change things on its own?

It drafts changes; it never confirms them. A person you name approves each one before it's treated as true.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Any tool that can read files. We set it up with the tools you use; the files themselves don't depend on any one of them.

What does it cost?

Each step is scoped to your business and quoted before work starts. The audit comes first, and tells us both whether the build is worth it.
